Champions VGC damage & teams

Overview

Damage calculation and team analysis for Pokémon Champions doubles.

Precise damage

See all 16 damage rolls, percentage ranges, and exact OHKO, 2HKO, and multi-hit KO odds, with spread reduction, Helping Hand, Friend Guard, weather, terrain, screens, abilities, items, and status applied.

Champions SP tools

Build with the Stat Point system: 66 SP total and up to 32 per stat. Find the minimum investment to survive an attack, secure a KO, or outspeed a target, or let the SP Optimizer balance bulk and speed.

Plan the whole team

Regulation M-C is selected throughout, with its 231-species roster. Find shared weaknesses and coverage gaps in Team Analysis, and import or export teams in Showdown format — offline, in English, 日本語, 简体中文, and 繁體中文.
