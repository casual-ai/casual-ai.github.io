Eight ready-made boards

Choose from the board gallery, set up solo or two-player play, and rotate the board to face your table. Available modes depend on your screen and window size.

Everyday match tools

Adjust damage, track all five Special Conditions, flip a coin and roll a real 3D die. Keep VSTAR Power and GX Attack use visible too.

Stay on turn

Track Supporter, Energy, Retreat and Stadium use for each player. End Turn clears that player's checklist; the adjustable match timer keeps practice moving.

Start free

Timer, Timer + Coin and Timer + Dice are free. One purchase unlocks all other boards, including future additions. No subscription; existing unlocks carry over.

One playmat for the whole match.

Unfold your table.

The unfolded iPhone Duo has room for Full Table and two-player Damage Counter. Fold it closed and large boards wait safely until you open it again.

Your iPad is the whole table.

Full Table brings every tool together. Full Table and two-player Damage Counter need a large screen and a window at least 600 × 800 points. If the window shrinks, widen it to resume with your game state intact.

Choose your tools, then play.

The gallery shows all eight boards, from a simple timer to Full Table. Set up each board for your game; its player mode, orientation and timer settings are remembered on your device.
