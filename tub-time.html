Tub Time

A bathtub full of toys, for winding down

Overview

Look down into the bath, drop the toys in, and play.

Toys that do things

They float, bump and drift when you tip the device. Tap one and it squeaks, spins, squirts, swims off, blows bubbles or jumps with a splash.

Something new each day

A few new toys turn up while you play, and every day brings its own bathtub. There are 132 toys and 30 bathtubs to meet, and a book that keeps count.

One price, nothing else

No in-app purchases, no ads, no accounts and no internet connection needed. Nothing to win and nothing to lose: run the tap, pull the plug, and take your time.

A quiet toy for the end of the day.
