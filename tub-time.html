Tub Time

A calm tub full of bath toys

Overview

Look down into the bath, drop the toys in, and play.

Toys that do things

They float, bump and drift when you tip the device. Tap one and it squeaks, spins, squirts, swims off, blows bubbles or jumps with a splash.

Something new each day

A few new toys turn up while you play, and every day brings its own bathtub. There are 150+ toys and 30 bathtubs to meet, and your toy box keeps count.

No ads, no account

No ads, no accounts and no internet connection needed to play. The capsule machine runs on coins that come only from time in the tub, and are never sold. Nothing to win and nothing to lose: run the tap, pull the plug, and take your time.
