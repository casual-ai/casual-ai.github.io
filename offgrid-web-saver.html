Overview

Instagram and X don't let you save photos. This does.

Right-click any photo and choose "Save to Offgrid Gallery." The extension finds the highest-resolution version of that exact photo and hands it to Offgrid Gallery on your Mac — no screenshots, no quality loss, nothing uploaded anywhere else. It works on any site with images, not just Instagram and X.

Setup

Three steps to get saving.

1. Open Offgrid Companion once

Download and open the app once. It registers the Safari extension and, if you use Chrome, connects it automatically — then you can quit it or leave it running, whichever you prefer.

2. Turn on Offgrid Saver in Safari

In the app, click "Open Safari Extension Settings," then turn on Offgrid Saver and allow it on the sites you use.

3. Or load it unpacked in Chrome

Download the Chrome version, unzip it, open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer Mode, then click "Load unpacked" and choose the unzipped folder.

The first time you save a photo, macOS may show a one-time prompt asking to allow access to a shared app container — this is expected. Choose Allow so the photo can reach Offgrid Gallery.

FAQ

Good to know.

Does this upload my photos anywhere?

No. The photo goes straight from your browser to Offgrid Gallery on the same Mac — nothing is sent to any server, including ours.

Which vault does it save to?

Whichever vault is active in Offgrid Gallery. If that vault has iCloud Drive turned on, the save is routed there automatically, the same as any other import.

Why do I need a separate app?

Browser extensions can't write files to another app on their own. Offgrid Companion is a small, notarized helper that lets Safari and Chrome hand the photo off to Offgrid Gallery.
