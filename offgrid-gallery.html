Offgrid Gallery

Fandom vaults & private albums

Overview

A photo and video vault for the things you love.

One vault for each thing you love

Make a vault for each idol, artist, anime, manga or game character, or hobby, with as many albums as you want in each. Save from any app with the share sheet and choose the vault it goes to.

Your favorites on your Home Screen

Put a photo, an album or a photo grid on your Home Screen, and a photo on your Lock Screen. Add picture frames and a wall behind them. A widget shows only what you choose for it.

Private when you want it

Optional App Lock with Face ID or Touch ID. Everything stays on your device: no sign-in, no servers, no tracking, no ads. iCloud Drive is optional, off by default, and set per vault.
