Privacy Policy

This page contains the privacy policy for Poképani.

Poképani does not collect, store or transmit personal data to developer-operated servers. Game state stays on your device; board setup and app settings are stored locally. Version 2.0 does not sync boards through iCloud and removes legacy 1.x custom board presets from the device and iCloud once. In-app purchases are processed by Apple, and the developer does not receive your payment details. Poképani does not use third-party analytics or user accounts.
