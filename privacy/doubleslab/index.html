Privacy Policy

This page contains the privacy policy for DoublesLab.

DoublesLab does not collect, store, or transmit personal data to developer-operated servers. Battle calculations, saved teams, imported sets, preferences, and app data are stored locally on your device. The bundled battle dataset is available offline. If iCloud sync is enabled on your device, supported app data may sync through Apple's iCloud service without passing through the developer's servers. In-app purchases are processed by Apple; the developer does not receive your payment details. DoublesLab does not use third-party analytics or user accounts.
