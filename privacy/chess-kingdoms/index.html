Privacy Policy

This page contains the privacy policy for Chess Kingdoms.

Chess Kingdoms does not collect, store, or transmit personal data to developer-operated servers. Game progress, saved matches, settings, puzzle progress, and tutorial progress are stored locally on your device. If iCloud sync is enabled on your device, supported game data may sync through Apple's iCloud service without passing through the developer's servers. In-app purchases are processed by Apple; the developer does not receive your payment details. Chess Kingdoms does not use third-party analytics or user accounts.
