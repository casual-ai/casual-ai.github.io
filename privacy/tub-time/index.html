Privacy Policy

This page contains the privacy policy for Tub Time.

Tub Time does not collect any personal data. It has no accounts of its own, shows no ads, contains no analytics or tracking, and plays without an internet connection. Your toys, bathtubs, settings and time played are stored on your device and are removed when you delete the app. If you turn on “Save to iCloud” in the game's settings, the toys and bathtubs you have found are also kept in your own iCloud account, through Apple, so that they come back after a reinstall and appear on your other devices. We cannot see or access them, and they remain in your iCloud after the app is deleted. The motion sensor is read only to move the water while you play, and nothing from it is kept.
