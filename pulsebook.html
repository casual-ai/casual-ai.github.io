Pulsebook

Blood pressure diary and tracker

Overview

Log a reading in seconds, and see where your blood pressure is heading.

Know your numbers

Your latest reading with its AHA/ACC category, shown in words as well as color, plain next steps for every reading, and a 7-day chart on the first screen.

See your trends

Charts from 30 days to all time, with averages, highs and lows, and your history grouped by month. Gentle reminders help you measure at the same times each day.

Private by design

Save readings to Apple Health, or keep them on this device only. No account, no ads and no tracking.

Screenshots

Your readings and your trend, at a glance.

Not a medical device. Talk to your doctor about your blood pressure.
